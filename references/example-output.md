## Default Three-Level Output

Prompt:

> eli5 materiality in an audit

Output:

**1. ELI5**
Materiality is like checking a grocery receipt. If apples show as $3.02 instead of $3.00, nobody cares. But if the receipt says you bought a $300 steak you never bought, that changes the whole bill.

**2. Freshman**
Materiality means "big enough to matter." In an audit, the auditor is not trying to prove every penny is perfect. The auditor is trying to catch mistakes that could change how someone reads or uses the financial statements.

**3. Entry-Level CPA**
Materiality is the threshold for whether an error or omission could reasonably influence a financial statement user's decision. It affects audit planning, testing, and how identified misstatements are evaluated. The practical idea is that the auditor focuses effort on things that could matter to users, not tiny differences that would not change the overall picture.

## Accounting Concept

Prompt:

> eli5 deferred revenue

Output:

**1. ELI5**
Deferred revenue is like getting paid today to bake a cake next week. You have the cash, but you still owe the cake. You count it as earned only after you do what you promised.

**2. Freshman**
Deferred revenue happens when a customer pays before the company has delivered the product or service. The company has money now, but it still owes something. So it records the amount as a liability until it finishes the job.

**3. Entry-Level CPA**
Deferred revenue is a liability recorded when cash is received before the related performance obligation is satisfied. The cash receipt does not automatically create revenue because the company still owes goods or services to the customer. As the company performs, it reduces deferred revenue and recognizes revenue. For exact GAAP treatment or timing, use an authoritative accounting or auditing review.

## Selected-Level Follow-Up

Prompt:

> 2

Output:

At the Freshman level: deferred revenue means "paid now, earned later."

Think of it as a promise tracker. The company already received the customer's money, but it has not finished what the customer paid for. Until the company delivers, it should not act like it fully earned the money. That is why deferred revenue sits as a liability: it represents work the company still owes.

Example: a customer pays $1,200 on January 1 for a one-year service plan. At the start, the company owes 12 months of service. After one month, it has earned 1 month and still owes 11 months.

Common mistake: people see cash come in and assume it is automatically revenue. It is not. Revenue depends on earning it, not just collecting it.

## Business Topic

Prompt:

> eli5 what SEO is

Output:

**1. ELI5**
SEO is helping people find your website when they search online. It is like putting clear signs on a store so people know what you sell. Good SEO helps Google understand your page and helps people find the answer they wanted.

**2. Freshman**
SEO means search engine optimization. It is the work you do so search engines can understand your website and show it to the right people. That usually means clear page titles, helpful content, fast pages, and words that match what people are searching for.

**3. Entry-Level Business**
SEO is an unpaid marketing channel focused on earning visibility in search results. It has three main parts: content that answers search intent, technical structure that lets search engines crawl and understand the site, and credibility signals from other reputable sites. The business goal is qualified traffic that can turn into leads, customers, or brand trust.

## General Topic

Prompt:

> eli5 golf handicap

Output:

**1. ELI5**
A golf handicap is a helper number that makes a game fair. If one player is much better, the other player gets extra strokes. That way both players can still have a real match.

**2. Freshman**
A golf handicap estimates how good a golfer is compared with other golfers. A lower handicap usually means the golfer is better. The number helps adjust scores so people with different skill levels can compete more fairly.

**3. College Grad**
A golf handicap is a standardized measure of a player's potential ability, not simply their average score. It uses recent scores and adjusts for course difficulty, then converts into a course handicap for the tees being played. The practical point is fairness: it estimates how many strokes a player should receive so golfers of different abilities can have a competitive match.
